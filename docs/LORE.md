# Lore Skeleton

## Premise
The galaxy is unstable. Trade routes are breaking down, frontier sectors go dark, and factions interpret the crisis in radically different ways.

## Faction placeholders
### 1. Core Federation
Order, law, logistics, survival of civilization.

### 2. Frontier Free Captains
Freedom, improvisation, private initiative, chaotic heroism.

### 3. Synthetic / Dominion Threat
Cold expansion, systems control, suppression of organic autonomy.

## Tone
Space adventure, weird signals, dark humor, political tension, personal choices.

## Story hooks
- missing convoy in a dead corridor
- signal from forbidden sector
- faction cover-up
- broken AI relics
- moral split between stability and freedom
