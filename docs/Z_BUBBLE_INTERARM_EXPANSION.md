# KR3 Z-Mechanic — Bubble Inter-Arm Travel

Status: canonical campaign and expansion architecture.

## Core rule

The **Bubble technology** is a mandatory late-campaign pillar of *Space Rangers 3: Children of Eltan*. It is not ordinary hyperspace and not a cosmetic fast-travel system. It creates a stabilized spacetime bubble/corridor capable of crossing distances between Galactic arms that ordinary ranger engines and standard hyperspace routes cannot traverse in campaign-relevant time.

The base KR3 campaign uses the Bubble as the technological bridge from the Coalition's starting arm to the **Klissan arm** and the **Eltan system**. Reaching Eltan is a major campaign threshold and must require actual story/science/Z-mechanic progression rather than a menu unlock.

## Progression toward the first Bubble

The first operational inter-arm Bubble should compose several existing KR3 systems:

- Keller/deep-hyperspace research and navigation data;
- Klissan/Mahpella biological or causal samples;
- black-hole / Shadow Fleet discoveries;
- Coalition science and station infrastructure;
- Children of Eltan story knowledge;
- player decisions in Smart Diplomacy and the three-layer Z campaign.

Different campaign routes may obtain the missing components in different ways. The exact Bubble project therefore becomes another convergence point for military, scientific, diplomatic, pirate, Keller and Children-of-Eltan play styles.

## Klissan arm

After the first successful Bubble transit, the galaxy map expands rather than replacing the original arm. The Klissan arm is a persistent second strategic theatre with its own:

- star systems, governments/settlements where lore permits, hostile territories and exploration sites;
- Klissan/Mahpella ecology and command network;
- Kelleroid, hyperpirate, Shadow Fleet and unknown-ship presence where Z-layer rules permit;
- bases, markets, anomalies, black holes and deep-hyper routes;
- faction story arcs and discoveries unavailable in the starting arm;
- dynamic war state and consequences that can propagate back to the original arm.

The **Eltan system** is the narrative/strategic heart of this theatre. It must be a real explorable system and not merely a finale cutscene location.

## 37 victory methods

The second-arm/Eltan campaign expands the current 36 designed finale families to **37 canonical base victory methods**. The existing 36 remain valid; the Bubble does not invalidate them.

### 37. Eltan Exodus / Inter-Arm Ascension

Complete the full Bubble project, reach Eltan, stabilize a permanent inter-arm corridor/network and resolve the Main Mahpella crisis by changing the strategic geometry of the war itself: evacuation, reinforcement, diplomacy and multi-arm logistics become possible on a civilization scale. Depending on accumulated world state, the player can use the Eltan network to preserve threatened populations, surround the Klissan network with allied infrastructure, open a durable diplomatic frontier, or force a strategic settlement without requiring extermination of either civilization.

This is the victory route most directly tied to mastery of the complete Z-mechanic. It requires substantial cross-layer exploration and Bubble infrastructure and should have several epilogue variants based on Keller, Mahpella, Coalition, pirate, hybrid-race and Shadow Fleet state.

## Post-game and addon architecture

The first Bubble is deliberately the beginning of a larger traversal system.

Base game:

`starting arm → Klissan arm → Eltan`

Later expansions/addons can unlock additional Milky Way arms through improved Bubble generations:

- Bubble Mk I — experimental one-way/limited inter-arm expedition;
- Bubble Mk II — repeatable stabilized connection between known arms;
- Bubble Mk III — player-selectable long-range inter-arm routes with infrastructure requirements;
- mature Bubble Network — strategic travel among multiple explored Milky Way arms.

Every added arm should be a genuine campaign region, not a palette swap: new system-generation profiles, political/ecological conditions, factions or subfactions, Z-layer topology, exploration content, resources, threats, quest chains and endings/epilogues.

## Gameplay constraints

- Bubble travel consumes strategic resources and/or infrastructure; it must not trivialize ordinary engines and hyperspace.
- Early Bubble travel is dangerous and can create playable transit incidents.
- A Bubble destination requires navigation/causality data; unexplored arms cannot simply be selected from a list.
- Bubble infrastructure can become a military, diplomatic and economic objective.
- Factions can fight over access, sabotage anchors, blockade endpoints or negotiate transit rights.
- Smart Diplomacy affects who may use a Bubble network and at what cost.
- Save state records discovered arms, routes, anchors, stability, ownership and Bubble technology generation.
- The Dynamic War Map eventually supports multiple Galactic arms plus their deep-hyper and shadow-world layers.

## Design principle

Ordinary hyperspace connects stars. The Z-mechanic connects layers of reality. **Bubble technology connects Galactic arms.** Together these systems form the long-range navigation identity of KR3.

The base game's journey to Eltan establishes the technology; later addons can turn the Milky Way itself into the long-term strategic map.
